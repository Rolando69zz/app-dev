# My Favorite Series

## Breaking Bad


**Genre:** Crime, Drama, Thriller

**Creator:** Vince Gilligan

**Summary:**  
Breaking Bad follows Walter White, a chemistry teacher who turns to a life of crime after a cancer diagnosis. With his former student Jesse Pinkman, he produces and sells crystal meth to secure his family's future.

### Why I Love It
- Amazing storytelling
- Brilliant character development
- Intense and unpredictable plot
- Incredible acting performances

> "I am the one who knocks." – Walter White
